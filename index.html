The story behind the ambition

A story still
being written.

“I am learning today for the professional — and the businesswoman — I hope to become tomorrow.”

Every meaningful journey begins before the destination is visible. Mine begins with learning. I am pursuing B.Sc. Physics Honours at D.N. College, Daudnagar, affiliated with Magadh University, and building my academic foundation one step at a time.

My Digital Marketing internship at IT Hub Solution, Patna, gave me an opportunity to connect learning with practical work. I gained exposure to SEO fundamentals, keyword research, social media scheduling, campaign metrics and reporting — and a clearer sense of how much there is still to learn.

Today, I am preparing for an Assistant Manager opportunity at State Bank of India. Alongside this preparation, I also guide and teach students. Sharing what I learn and helping others understand their studies keeps me curious, strengthens my communication and reminds me that progress becomes more meaningful when it is shared.

Banking is an important next step, but it is not the limit of my ambition. My bigger dream is to become a businesswoman and build an enterprise of my own — with thoughtful decisions, responsibility and purpose at its heart.

I know the journey will ask me to keep learning, adapt and grow. I am ready to take it one focused step at a time.
